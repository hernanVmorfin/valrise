# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a dependency-light Cloudflare Worker with static assets for the preview, followed by the existing native WordPress block theme for production.

## Users

Executives, founders, business owners, high-potential leaders, and organizations looking for executive advisory, leadership development, women's leadership, or a speaker.

## Product Purpose

Help a prospective client quickly understand VALRISE Consulting's focus, see whether the work fits the decision or transition in front of them, and begin a qualified consultation or speaking inquiry.

## Positioning

VALRISE connects personal clarity with practical business judgment for leaders carrying consequential decisions and meaningful transitions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English-first launch.
- Primary action: “Book a consultation.”
- Cloudflare Worker is the first live preview; WordPress remains the intended production CMS.
- Inquiry delivery must not appear operational until a real recipient or webhook is configured.
- Biography, credentials, client names, outcomes, testimonials, event history, and statistics remain unverified unless supplied and approved.
- Privacy and Terms content remains subject to legal review.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VALRISE Consulting.
- Approved identity: black VAL / blue RISE, blue `#3DA2EF`, black supporting text, and the supplied sculpted-partnership logo system.
- Voice: clear, candid, practical, calm, and executive-level; avoid hype and generic coaching language.
- Established editorial visual system: warm paper, charcoal, blue accents, serif display typography, restrained lines, and square-cornered controls.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved logo exports under `output/website-logo-pack/`.
- Existing WordPress theme, copy, information architecture, and editorial constraints under `packages/valrise/`.
- Seven user-supplied portrait and event photos are available as website assets. Their final placement, captions, and any claims inferred from them remain subject to approval.
- No approved testimonials, client logos, quantified results, or public speaking claims are present in the workspace.

## Product Principles

- Clarity before prescription.
- Specific usefulness over broad inspiration.
- Show only claims that can be supported.
- Make the next step direct and dignified.
- Keep the preview portable into WordPress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layouts, semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and form recovery that does not depend on a mouse.
