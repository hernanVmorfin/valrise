Perspective shaped by responsibility, reinvention, and the work of leading well.

Valeria Mogildea is the founder of VALRISE Consulting, an executive advisory and leadership development firm for leaders navigating consequential decisions and meaningful transition.

A practical point of view.

VALRISE is built on a simple belief: the strongest leadership work connects personal clarity to practical business judgment.

After coming to the United States as a teenager, Valeria built an insurance agency from the ground up, developed it into a thriving company, and ultimately completed a successful exit. Her experience in Corporate America adds a second perspective: entrepreneurial agility informed by disciplined strategic thinking.

Today, she helps leaders examine the decision beneath the noise, communicate with greater intention, and move toward their next level with a practical plan.

How the work is approached.

Clarity

Name what matters before deciding what to do.

Candor

Make room for honest perspective without losing respect or discretion.

Practicality

Translate insight into decisions, communication, and useful action.
